Frequently asked questions

How do I use add & edit plants?

Add a plant with a name, room and plant type. The type preset fills in a sensible watering interval that you can adjust from 1 to 60 days.

How do I use today list?

See every plant that is due or overdue, most urgent first, and mark it watered with one tap.

How do I use plant detail & history?

Each plant shows its next due date, interval, room, and a history of past waterings.

How do I use care streak?

A streak counts consecutive days on which every due plant was watered.

How do I use upcoming week?

The next 7 days of watering, grouped by day.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with your iOS version, the app version (shown in Settings) and what happened.
