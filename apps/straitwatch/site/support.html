Frequently asked questions

How do I use tap to intercept?

Ships cross the strait left to right while drones, ballistic and guided missiles, jets and boats attack from the shore. Tap a threat to fire the carrier's gun at it; a rack of rounds reloads over time. Losing too many ships ends the run.

How do I use 20 maps in four chapters?

Home Waters, The Narrows, Dark Water and Open War: five maps each, unlocked in order by clearing the one before. Each map after the first five adds one new idea: a bending lane, an allied radar, a supertanker, single file, lock gates, shore batteries, drifting mines, oil rigs, night, a battleship, fast boats, a fork, two-way traffic, the tide and the Citadel.

How do I use twelve weapons?

After each wave the player drafts one of three weapons (Flak Cannon, Seeker Pod, Aegis Launchers, Gravity Well, Air Strike, Railgun, Tesla Arc, Shrapnel Shell, Frigate Escort, Thermobaric Shell, Scattergun, Boomerang). Held weapons drop as pickups from kills; tapping one loads the gun with it for 15 seconds. Three held at once, three levels each; level 3 is MAX with a new name.

How do I use the armory?

Eleven upgrade cards in three bays (Gun, Hull, Supply), won at random at the end of every run; a duplicate adds a level. Before each run the player equips one card from each bay.

How do I use endless mode?

After clearing a map the run can continue into endless waves that keep growing.

How do I use game center?

A best-score leaderboard for every map and achievements for milestones, through Apple Game Center.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with your iOS version, the app version (shown in Settings) and what happened.
